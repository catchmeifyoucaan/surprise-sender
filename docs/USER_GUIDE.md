# User Guide

This guide provides instructions for using the Surprise Sender application.

## Getting Started

1. **Register**: Create a new account by clicking the "Register" button on the login page.
2. **Login**: Log in to your account using your email and password.
3. **Dashboard**: After logging in, you'll be taken to the dashboard, where you can see an overview of your email activity.

## Sending Emails

- **Single Email**: To send a single email, click the "Compose" button in the sidebar.
- **Bulk Email**: To send a bulk email, click the "Bulk Email" button in the sidebar. You'll need to upload a CSV or XLSX file with a list of recipients.
- **Templated Email**: To send an email using a template, click the "Templates" button in the sidebar. You can create new templates or use one of the pre-built templates.

## AI Agents

- **Create an Agent**: To create a new AI agent, click the "Agents" button in the sidebar.
- **Create a Crew**: To create a new crew of agents, click the "Crews" button in the sidebar.
- **Assign a Task**: To assign a task to a crew, click the "Tasks" button in the sidebar.
